01Get help

This page provides support for apps that link to it from within the app or their App Store product page. Use the email address above for questions, technical problems, privacy requests, accessibility issues, or suggestions.

Please identify the app you are asking about. If a mail app does not open when you select the address, copy it into your preferred email service. If you have already contacted us about the same issue, reply to the existing email thread to add information.

For a useful starting point, see Troubleshooting or the problem-report checklist. For a purchase billed by Apple, see Purchases and billing.

02Troubleshooting

If an app is not behaving as expected, start with these steps:

  1. Save your work. Use any save or export controls the app provides before closing it or restarting your device.
  2. Check for an app update. Look for an available update on the app's App Store product page, and check its compatibility information.
  3. Reopen the app. If the issue continues, restart your device and try the same action again.
  4. Check relevant device settings. Make sure there is available storage and, if a feature needs a permission, review that permission in Settings.
  5. Check the connection for online features. If the affected feature requires internet access, try again with a working connection. This step does not apply to features that work entirely on your device.

Before deleting, reinstalling, or resetting an app: these actions can remove local information. Preserve any data you need first. If you are unsure what will be removed, contact us before proceeding.

If the problem continues, send the details in the next section. You do not need to repeat steps that could put your work at risk.

03Report a problem

A useful email subject is the app's name followed by a short description of the issue. Include whichever of the following details you can:

  • App name and version. Use the version shown in the app's About or Settings screen, if available.
  • Device and system version. Tell us the device model and operating-system version.
  • Steps to reproduce. Describe what you did before the issue occurred.
  • Expected and actual results. Explain what you expected to happen and what happened instead, including any visible error message.
  • Frequency and recent changes. Note whether it happens every time and whether it began after an update or a settings change.
  • Optional screenshots or recordings. Attach them only if helpful, with personal information and unrelated content removed or obscured.

Do not send passwords, verification codes, full payment-card details, or information that is unnecessary to explain the issue. Any diagnostic material you include in an email is information you choose to share; contacting support does not give us remote access to your device or its local app data.

04Compatibility and features

Device requirements, supported operating systems, languages, features, and pricing depend on the app. Check the corresponding App Store product page and the information in the app for the version you use.

If a feature looks different or is unavailable on your device, include the app name, version, device model, and the feature you expected when contacting us. Do not assume that instructions or capabilities for one app apply to another.

05Purchases and billing

This section applies only if your issue concerns a paid download, in-app purchase, or subscription. It does not mean that every app offers purchases or subscriptions.

Restore eligible purchases

If the app offers a restore-purchases control, use it while signed in to the Apple Account used for the original purchase. Some purchases, including consumable items, cannot be restored. See Apple's help with missing in-app purchases for the applicable steps.

Manage or cancel a subscription

For a subscription billed by Apple, use the subscription settings for your Apple Account. Deleting an app does not cancel its subscription. Follow Apple's subscription cancellation instructions to review or stop a renewal.

Request a refund or question a charge

Apple reviews refund requests for purchases billed by Apple. You can report a problem or request a refund from Apple; eligibility depends on Apple's policies and applicable law. See Apple's refund guidance for details. We cannot guarantee a refund outcome.

If another provider billed your purchase, contact that provider using the details on your receipt. For an app feature that remains unavailable after an eligible purchase, email us with the app name and a description of the issue; avoid including full payment details.

06Privacy and data

The Privacy Policy describes support correspondence, website visits, and apps that follow its stated on-device practices. For any additional data processing, also read the privacy notice provided by the app or feature.

Local information and backups

Use available in-app delete or reset controls to manage saved information. Deleting an app generally removes its local app container; offloading or archiving it may preserve its data. Device or cloud backups, exported files, and information shared with another service need to be managed separately.

We cannot remotely retrieve, restore, or erase information held only on your device. If you need help identifying the relevant local controls, tell us the app name and the type of information involved. See the policy's data-deletion guidance.

Privacy requests and support messages

Email the support address above to ask about information we hold, request deletion of support correspondence, or exercise an applicable privacy right. Include enough context to locate your request without sending unnecessary sensitive information. The policy explains retention and privacy rights.

Accounts, where available

If the app supports creating an account, use its in-app account-deletion process to initiate deletion. Contact us if you need help locating or using it. Removing an app from your device is different from deleting an account or cancelling a subscription.

07Accessibility and feedback

For an accessibility issue, describe the screen or task where you encountered it and what made it difficult to use. If relevant, you may include the assistive feature you were using, such as VoiceOver, larger text, keyboard navigation, or a display accommodation.

For a feature suggestion, explain what you are trying to do and how the proposed change would help. Send feedback to kingla0810@outlook.com, and include the app's name so we can understand the context.
