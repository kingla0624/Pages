01About this policy

This policy applies to applications that link to it from within the app or their app-store listing (each an “App”) and follow the on-device practices described here. It also covers related support correspondence and visits to this policy page. “We,” “us,” and “our” refer to the publisher of the App that directed you to this policy.

Personal information means information that identifies you, or can reasonably be linked to you. This policy distinguishes information that stays on your device from information we receive when you contact us and technical information used to serve this website.

If an App provides a separate privacy notice for a feature, that notice explains the additional information involved, how and why it is used, its recipients, retention, and your choices. It is presented before that feature processes the information and should be read together with this policy. A general reference to a feature or a system permission prompt does not authorize undisclosed data collection.

02App data and local processing

The Apps covered by this policy process their content on your device. The App does not send your content, personal identifiers, usage events, or crash diagnostics to us or to service providers acting on our behalf. It does not require an account or include advertising, tracking, or third-party analytics or crash-reporting services.

Depending on the features you use, local app storage can contain:

  • Content and saved work: information you enter, create, import, or choose to save.
  • Settings and preferences: choices that allow the App to remember how you use its features.
  • Local working data: information needed to operate a feature or resume your work.

We cannot remotely access information held only in local app storage. System backups and any information you choose to export or share are handled separately, as described below.

03Support correspondence

When you email us, we receive your email address, any name shown in the message, the message contents and attachments, and technical details you choose to include, such as the App version, device model, or operating-system version.

We use this information to:

  • Answer questions and troubleshoot the problem you report.
  • Handle privacy requests and communicate the outcome.
  • Keep records needed for follow-up, resolving disputes, security, or legal obligations.

Support correspondence is voluntary and separate from normal app use. Do not send passwords or sensitive information that is unnecessary for your request. We use Microsoft Outlook to receive and store email; see the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

Where applicable law requires a legal basis, we rely on our legitimate interests in responding to support requests and maintaining necessary records, subject to your rights; legal obligations when handling legally required requests or records; and consent where that is required.

04Visits to this website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Opening this page sends a request to GitHub, which may process connection information such as your IP address, browser and operating-system details, the requested address, the request time, and a referring address if your browser provides one. GitHub uses technical information to deliver and secure the website and may maintain logs under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

This page does not set cookies or include advertising, tracking pixels, or third-party analytics. Reading the policy does not give us access to your App's local content. Website requests and hosting logs are separate from the App's on-device processing.

05Sharing and service providers

We do not sell or rent personal information, or share it for targeted or cross-context behavioral advertising. We disclose information we hold only:

  • To necessary service providers, such as Microsoft for email and GitHub for website hosting, for the purposes described in this policy.
  • At your direction or with your permission, when you ask us to share information.
  • For legal and security reasons, when required by law or reasonably necessary to protect rights, safety, or security, limited to what is required or permitted by law.

The App does not send its locally stored content to these providers. Microsoft processes support correspondence, and GitHub processes website connection information, as described above. Before engaging a third party to receive App user data on our behalf, we require it to provide the same or equal protection as this policy and the applicable App Store privacy requirements, and to use the data only for the disclosed purposes. We remain responsible for explaining how providers we engage handle information on our behalf.

Services you use independently

Your operating system, app store, device-backup provider, or a destination you choose when sharing content may process information independently under its own privacy policy. For example, app data may be included in a device or cloud backup, and a platform may collect diagnostics according to your settings. For Apple services, see the Apple Privacy Policy.

A system backup is different from an App providing its own cloud synchronization. You manage backups and platform diagnostic-sharing choices through the relevant device or service settings. When you export or share content, the destination receives the information you select.

06Permissions and consent

If an App feature requests access to a device capability, the permission prompt explains its purpose. You can decline optional access or change permissions in your device settings. The feature needing that permission may then be unavailable. A device permission alone does not mean information is transmitted to us.

Where processing relies on your consent, you can withdraw it through the relevant App control or by contacting us. Withdrawal does not affect processing that was lawful before you withdrew consent. Withdrawing consent or revoking a permission does not automatically delete information already stored; see Deleting your data.

We explain any new processing before it begins and obtain the permission required for it. This includes clearly disclosing and obtaining explicit permission before sharing personal information with a third-party service, including a third-party AI service. The App practices above do not include such sharing.

07Retention and security

Local app information

Local retention depends on the App's features, your choices, and your device's storage management. Saved information can remain until you remove it, while temporary information may be cleared by the App or operating system. We do not hold a server copy of information stored only on your device.

Information we receive

We keep support correspondence while handling your request and afterwards only as needed for necessary follow-up, resolving disputes, security, or legal obligations. When those reasons no longer apply, we delete or anonymize it. You may request deletion at any time; we will explain any information we must retain and why.

Hosting logs and provider-managed backups are subject to the relevant provider's retention practices described in its privacy statement. Deleting an active record may not immediately remove copies in provider backups.

Protecting information

We use reasonable safeguards appropriate to the information we handle. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure. Keeping your device updated and using its security controls helps protect locally stored information.

08Deleting your data

  • Saved app information: use available delete or reset controls in the App. Deleting the App generally removes its local app container. Offloading or archiving an App may preserve its data.
  • Backups and exported copies: manage these separately through your device, cloud-storage provider, or the destination where you saved or shared the information. Deleting an App does not necessarily delete those copies.
  • Information sent to us: email kingla0810@outlook.com to request deletion of support correspondence or other personal information we hold. We may need limited information to locate the record and verify your request.

The Apps covered by this policy do not create an account or server-side profile to delete. For information held only on your device, use the local controls above; we cannot retrieve or remotely erase it.

09Your privacy rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, your rights may include:

  • Access to, a copy of, or correction of personal information we hold.
  • Deletion, portability, restriction of processing, or an objection to processing.
  • Withdrawal of consent where processing is based on consent.
  • A complaint to the data-protection authority responsible for your region.

To exercise a right, contact us and describe your request. We request only information reasonably necessary to verify your identity and locate relevant records, and respond within the period required by applicable law. If we cannot fully fulfill a request because of a lawful exception, we will explain the reason. We will not discriminate against you for exercising applicable privacy rights.

10Children's privacy

Age suitability depends on the App; refer to its app-store listing. The on-device practices above do not give us access to a child's local app information.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, or under another age requiring parental consent in their jurisdiction, without the required authorization. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action, including deletion where required.

11International processing

Support email and website connection information may be processed in countries other than where you live, depending on the infrastructure used by Microsoft and GitHub. Their privacy statements describe processing locations and international data-transfer safeguards.

Where applicable law restricts an international transfer we make, we use the safeguards it requires. Contact us for information about transfers relevant to information we hold. This does not mean that locally stored App content is transferred to us.

12Changes to this policy

We publish revisions at this address and update the effective date. If a change materially affects how personal information is handled, we provide an appropriate notice through the affected App or another suitable channel before the new processing begins, and obtain consent where required.

A policy update or continued use of an App does not, by itself, provide consent for processing that requires your permission.
