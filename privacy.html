Privacy Policy

Option AI  ·  Effective 5 September 2026

Option AI does not collect any data. The app has no network access, no account, no analytics and no advertising. Everything you enter stays on your device.

Data we collect

None. The app does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data, usage data, identifiers, or diagnostics. There is no sign-up, no login, and no user account of any kind.

Network access

The app makes no network connections. It contains no networking code and no third-party SDKs, so there is no server for your information to reach — not ours, and not anyone else's.

What is stored on your device

So that the app remembers your work between launches, the following is saved locally in the app's own private container on your iPhone:

  • Market settings — the underlying name you type, base price, volatility, interest rate, dividend yield, and your implied-volatility surface.
  • Your custom strategies — their names, legs, and creation dates.
  • Saved positions — the legs and theoretical entry values of positions you save to the app's local scratchpad.
  • Interface preferences — display options such as the per-contract toggle, and the state of the built-in price simulation.

This information never leaves your device. It is not backed up to any service by the app itself; it may be included in your own iPhone or iCloud device backup, which is controlled entirely by you in iOS Settings and is governed by Apple's privacy policy, not ours.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app from your iPhone permanently removes everything it stored. You can also clear individual items inside the app by deleting your custom strategies and saved positions, or by resetting market settings.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party software development kits, no advertising networks, no analytics or crash-reporting services, and no tracking technologies. No data is shared with anyone, because none is collected.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13. There is no user-generated content, no messaging, and no way for one user to reach another.

About the data shown in the app

This is not a privacy matter, but it is worth stating plainly: the underlying price in Option AI is produced by a simulation running on your device. It is not market data. All option values, Greeks and probabilities are theoretical model outputs. The app is a study and analysis tool; it provides no brokerage service, executes no trades, and offers no investment advice.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the revised version will be posted at this address with a new effective date. Because the app collects nothing, we do not expect substantive changes unless the app itself gains a feature that requires them.
